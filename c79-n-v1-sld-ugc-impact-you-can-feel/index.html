Video Overview

We want this video built around this: as a coach, if there is only one thing you work on in your golf swing, it should be this. It is what you have found from studying and working with all the best. Everyone swings it differently, but this is the one thing, and it is impact position. Then you share the tool you have been using to train it.

Video Structure

The one idea this video needs to land: most golfers practice the wrong position thousands of times. The Slider makes the right position something you feel on every single ball, without thinking about it.

Your words, your style, but keep this order. The whole video should run 90 to 110 seconds. Reference clips sit inside each scene.

Scene 1 · The Hook (0 to 10 seconds)

Film all three, best first:

CHold the Slider up, look at it: "My students ask me all the time, why does my swing feel fine on the range but fall apart on the course? I tell them the same thing every time." Pause. "You've never actually felt the right position."

AOn the range with the Slider on, straight down the lens: "Most golf training aids are a waste of money. I've tried them all. This one's different, and I'll show you exactly why."

BHit a ball. We freeze it at impact in the edit. "That position right there, that's the only one that matters. And most golfers never feel it."

Scene 2 · The Problem (10 to 30 seconds)

Open with who you are, one line while the Slider is already on your arm: "I'm a PGA coach at St Mellion." Don't explain your credentials beyond that. The Slider on your arm does the work.

Then the problem, in plain words: "Here's the thing about impact. You can't see it. You can't feel it in real time. By the time the ball leaves the club, it's already done. So when your coach tells you to get your trail arm into a better position at impact, how are you supposed to feel what that even means?"

The key line: "That's why swing fixes don't stick. You're trying to consciously control something that happens in a fraction of a second."

Keep this under 20 seconds. One clear "oh, that's why" moment. Not a lecture.

Scene 3 · The Demo (30 to 80 seconds). This is the video, everything else is setup.

1Exaggerate the fault. Swing without the Slider, trail arm flying out, really show it. "Watch what happens when the trail arm disconnects." Show the result, weak and off-path.

2Put the Slider on. Casual, quick. "This is what changes it." No big setup speech.

3Swing with it on. Pause mid-follow-through or point to the contact point. "Feel that? That's the Power Slot. That's where every tour pro makes contact. The Slider just made it impossible to miss it."

4Take it off and swing again. "Now I take it off, and the feeling stays. That's the whole point. It's not a crutch. It's a teacher."

This should feel like a coaching session, not a product pitch. Talk through what you're feeling and seeing.

Scene 4 · The Results (80 to 90 seconds, three sentences max)

"What my students notice first is the strike. Ball comes off the middle more often. Then the consistency, same flight, same shape, round after round. And then, yeah, when you stop fighting the club and start compressing it properly, the distance takes care of itself."

On distance: say it in your own words from what you've actually seen with students. No specific number.
