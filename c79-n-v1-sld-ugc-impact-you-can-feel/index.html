Video Overview

We want this video built around this: as a coach, if there is only one thing you work on in your golf swing, it should be this. It is what you have found from studying and working with all the best. Everyone swings it differently, but this is the one thing, and it is impact position. Then you share the tool you have been using to train it.

Video Structure

Your words, your style, but keep this order. Timings are a rough guide. Reference clips sit inside each scene so you never have to hunt for them.

Pick one opener and riff on it:

A"Most golf training aids are a waste of money…" then raise the Slider on "this one's different".

B"Every swing looks different. Impact doesn't." Freeze on your own impact position if you can.

Scene 2 · Who You Are (5 to 10 seconds)

You're a golf coach at St Mellion. Say it early and keep it to one line. The on-screen text will do the rest.

Scene 3 · The Problem (10 to 25 seconds)

The main thing you work on with students is impact position. Backswings can look completely different, but the shot is decided at impact.

The problem is that you can't see your own impact, and most golfers can't feel it either. That's why swing fixes don't stick.

Scene 4 · The Demo (25 to 45 seconds). This carries the video.

Tell them this is the best tool you've found for training impact. Then show it. Strap it on, on camera, and hit balls.

Exaggerate the fault first, with your arms separating from the body. Then show the Slider physically holding your trail arm connected into impact. That's the position tour players hit, the Power Slot.

Film for this scene: strapping the Slider on in close-up, full swings with it on (face-on and down the line, slow-mo into impact), and the fault exaggerated with nothing on.
