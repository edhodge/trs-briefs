Video Overview

You turn away most training aids that come through your lesson bay. Say that on camera in your own words, then bring a real student whose trail arm comes away at the top, put the Slider on him, and let the strike do the rest.

Hooks To Film

H1

Every gimmick going has come through this lesson bay. I’ve told people to put most of them away.

You in the bay, Slider in hand, not on yet. Get your name and Teaching Professional on screen inside the first second.

H2

Here’s what I’m looking for as a coach when I’m analysing a training aid.

You straight down the lens, Slider held up. Flat delivery, you’re assessing it, not selling it.

H3

I thought this was just another strap. I was wrong.

Tight on the strap in your hand, turning it over. Hard cut to a swing with it on.

Scenes

Don’t have to match exact wording, keep to your own style.

S1

When I’m looking at a training aid, I’m looking for a couple of things. Can you hit balls with it? Is it realistic? Does it give the player feedback in the swing, not after it? Most don’t get past that.

You to camera holding the Slider, not wearing it yet.

S2

The only reason I opened this one is that it’s designed by Robert Rock, multiple winner on the European Tour. And there’s over fifty tour pros using it.

Robert Rock swing footage, then the tour pro shots. Put the names on screen: Robert Rock, Cam Smith, Tom Detry. A pro nobody can name counts for nothing.

S3

This is [student]. Plays off fifteen. Watch the trail arm — it comes away from his body at the top, and by the time he gets down to the ball he’s throwing the arms at it. Strike moves all over the face.

Film a REAL STUDENT, not yourself — we need a second person on camera, and we need their permission to film them. Their swing down the line, slow motion, line drawn on the trail arm at the top.

S4

This is when he’s wearing the Slider. It keeps that trail arm connected.

Close up of it going on, in real time. Then the same student down the line with it on. Freeze at impact and put The Power Slot on screen.

S5

Notice how the strike is different, that the Slider is automatically getting him into that correct slot.

Same student, same camera position as scene three so the two can be cut side by side. Let the ball and the sound of the strike do the work. Catch his reaction, don’t ask him for one.

S6

I’m not easily sold on training aids, but this one clearly forces people into the right positions.

You to camera. Then the proof on screen one at a time: 50,000+ golfers, 4.8 out of 5, designed by Robert Rock, 60-Day Lower Score Guarantee. Then a real customer review card.

S7

As a coach, I can confidently recommend this to anyone looking to improve their game.

The end card carries the offer. Don’t read a guarantee or a price out loud — this is your review, not an advert.
